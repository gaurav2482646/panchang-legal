# Privacy Policy — Panchang: Hindu Calendar

**Effective date: 17 August 2026**

Panchang: Hindu Calendar ("the app") is published by Gaurav Jeet Singh
("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles on Android and
iOS. The short version: your panchang is computed **on your device**; we run
no servers of our own and never sell your data.

## Location

The app's purpose is astronomically accurate Hindu calendar timings **for your
exact location**. To do that it uses your device's location (precise and
approximate), only while you use the app, and only if you grant permission.

- Your coordinates are stored **on your device** and used there to compute
  sunrise, tithi, muhurat and festival timings. They are not uploaded to us —
  we have no servers.
- Location is **optional**: you can instead search for your city by name, and
  the app uses that city's published coordinates.
- If a calculation fails and a diagnostic report is sent (see Crash reports
  below), any coordinate attached is first rounded to one decimal place —
  roughly an 11 km area — never your exact position.

## Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used (for
example, that a festival screen was opened). These events contain no precise
location, no names, and no contact details. Firebase's own processing is
described in Google's privacy documentation.

## Crash reports

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports so we
can fix defects. Reports include device model, OS version, and app state at
the time of the failure. As above, coordinates in these reports are rounded
to ~11 km before leaving the device.

## Advertising (free tier)

The free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob. **This version of the app
requests non-personalized ads only** — ads are never targeted using a profile
of your interests. Where consent regulations apply (for example the EEA and
UK), the app still presents Google's consent dialog before any ad is loaded.
AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier for purposes such as
limiting how often you see the same ad and preventing fraud.

- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in
  Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **iOS:** the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and
  does not track you across other companies' apps; you can additionally limit
  ad personalization in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Premium subscribers see no ads.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS).
We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat solely to check your
subscription status so the app can unlock premium features.

## What we do NOT collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, calendars, photos, files,
microphone audio, health data, or messages. The app has no account system.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all on-device data (your locations, settings and
cached calculations). To request deletion of analytics or crash data tied to
your device's identifiers, email us at **appsbygaurav@gmail.com** and we will
forward the deletion request to Firebase.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children
under 13.

## Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and update the effective
date above.

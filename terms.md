## The service

The app computes Hindu calendar information (tithi, nakshatra, muhurat,
festival dates and related timings) for the location you choose, using
high-precision astronomical calculations and established panchang methodology
(described in the app's Methodology screen). Timings are provided for religious and informational use.
While accuracy is the app's core commitment, calculations can differ between
traditions and sources; consult your own religious authority for observances
where the exact moment is critical to you.

# Support — Panchang: Hindu Calendar

Thanks for using Panchang. For any problem — wrong-looking timings, purchase
or subscription issues, reminder problems, or feature requests — email:

**appsbygaurav@gmail.com**

Please include your city, device model, and app version (shown at the bottom
of Settings). For timing questions, a screenshot of the day in question helps
us compare against reference sources quickly.

## Common questions

**Why do my dates differ from a printed panchang from India?**
The app computes timings for *your* location. A festival or tithi can
legitimately fall on a different civil day in California or London than in
India — that location accuracy is the app's purpose. The Methodology screen
in the app explains the calculation rules.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. iOS: Settings → your name →
Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**How do I change my city?**
Tap the "Calculated for …" badge on the Today screen, or Settings → Location.
